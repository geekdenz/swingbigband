# Manawatu Jazz Club Big Swing Band

The band is a jazz big swing band in Palmerston North, in the Manawatu, New Zealand, near Wellington. It plays under the Manawatu Jazz Club and is led by **Trevor Castle**.

</section>

<section class="about-details" markdown>

## The players

The line-up includes trumpet, saxophone, trombone, clarinet, drums, guitar, bass, and piano/keyboard players.

# The Sax Section Sound

A big band sax section works best when the line sounds like one instrument with five colors. That means matching phrase lengths before worrying about volume, then letting the lead alto define the front edge of the time.

For this book, the goal is a warm section blend on ballads and a leaner, more percussive attack on the faster swing charts. The difference should be obvious without feeling like a different band has walked on stage.

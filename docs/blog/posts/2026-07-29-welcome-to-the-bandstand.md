# Welcome to the Bandstand

SwingBig.Band now has a home for the details that usually disappear after rehearsal: the section notes, gig announcements, set-list changes, listening references, and stories that make a big band feel alive.

The blog will carry short posts first. Expect rehearsal takeaways, notes on charts entering the book, player features, and quick recaps after public sets.

## This Week's Focus
